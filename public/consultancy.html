Use Cases

Real problems, proven solutions

AI-powered

Intelligent Workflow Engine

Problem

A growing operations team was drowning in inbound requests routed manually by a coordinator. Misrouting caused delays, duplicated work, and frustrated staff — with no visibility into volume or patterns.

Solution

Built an LLM-backed classification and routing engine that reads each inbound request, determines the correct team or automated handler, and dispatches it instantly. Integrated with existing email and ticketing infrastructure with no workflow disruption during rollout.

Outcome

60% reduction in average response time. Eliminated the manual routing role, freeing the coordinator for higher-value work. Leadership gained full visibility into request volume and patterns for the first time.

Tech: Claude API, Webhooks, Node.js
AI-powered

SMB Operations Assistant

Problem

A 25-person professional services firm had institutional knowledge scattered across Slack threads, Google Drive folders, and individual inboxes. New hires took months to get up to speed and senior staff spent hours a week answering repeat operational questions.

Solution

Deployed an AI agent connected to Slack and Google Workspace via RAG pipelines. The agent answers operational questions, summarizes reports on demand, and drafts routine communications — drawing from the firm's own documents and conversation history as its knowledge base.

Outcome

Senior staff reclaimed an estimated 4–6 hours per week. New hire ramp time dropped significantly. The firm's collective knowledge became instantly searchable and actionable without any change to existing tools.

Tech: RAG, Slack API, Google Drive, Claude API
Legacy modernization

Oracle EPM Modernization

Problem

A finance team was running monthly close on a decade-old Oracle EPM reporting stack held together with manual exports, Excel transforms, and undocumented tribal knowledge. Every close cycle carried risk and consumed days of analyst time.

Solution

Conducted a full audit of the existing stack and redesigned the architecture with incremental ETL pipelines and a modern BI layer on top. Migration was staged to avoid disrupting live finance operations, with parallel running during the transition period.

Outcome

Monthly close cycle shortened by 3 days. Report generation moved from hours to minutes. Tribal knowledge was documented and encoded into automated processes, eliminating single points of failure.

Tech: Oracle EPM, ETL, Power BI, SQL
