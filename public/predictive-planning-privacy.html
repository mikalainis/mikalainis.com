Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Mikalogix LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Predictive Planning Google Sheets Add-on. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data Collection and Scopes

To provide its services, Predictive Planning requires access to your Google Sheets data. We use the following OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly: To read the data you select for analysis and forecasting.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email: To identify your account for subscription management and support.

2. How Data is Used

Your spreadsheet data is accessed solely to perform the planning and prediction functions requested by you within the add-on. We do not use your spreadsheet data for any other purpose.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or trade your data with any third parties. All data processing occurs within the context of providing the requested service to you.

4. Data Retention

We do not store your spreadsheet data on our servers. Any data read from your spreadsheets is processed in-memory and is not retained after the specific analysis task is complete.

5. User Rights

You have the right to revoke access to the Service at any time through your Google Account settings. You may also request deletion of any account-related information (such as your email address) by contacting us.

6. Google API Compliance

Predictive Planning's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data during processing.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at pmikalainis@gmail.com.
